My current research focuses on **AI for Scientific Discovery**, spanning reliable code reproduction from academic papers and the use of reinforcement learning to help LLM-based agents propose more novel scientific ideas and predict promising research directions. I previously worked as a Research Engineer at Meta Superintelligence Labs, where I contributed to the [Muse Glimmer](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model) and [Muse Spark](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-msl/) models.

Muse Glimmer is an open-source 30B-parameter model designed for autonomous agents running locally on consumer hardware. It accepts text and image inputs and combines multi-step reasoning, reliable tool use, and failure recovery. Distillation from Muse Spark, 4-bit quantization, and speculative decoding enable efficient on-device inference. The model is released under the Apache 2.0 license.

I am listed as a **core contributor** in Section 11.1 of the technical report (page 14).
